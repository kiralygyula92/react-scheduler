Add the React components: `Scheduler`, `ListView` and `TimelineView`, with pin-on-pass and the pinned strip, shift navigation, the time grid with the now indicator, the overflow and item detail dialogs (loaded lazily), and loading, empty and error states. Every one of the 55 parts can be replaced or restyled through `slots`, `slotProps`, `classNames` and `styles`; render props, handler middleware, events, an imperative handle and headless hooks (`useScheduler` and focused hooks) cover custom markup. Styles ship as `styles.css` (classic and default presets, light and dark schemes, three densities) and `base.css` (structure only). The DOM engines are available from `/dom`, and the framework-agnostic `createScheduler` from `/core`.
