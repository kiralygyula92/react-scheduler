Add the headless core, available from `@react-schedulerkit/react-scheduler/core`: the shift model (regular and pattern shifts, DST-safe wall-clock boundaries), bucketing of items into shifts, the timeline layout engine, locale-aware formatters, localization with plural rules, time labels, and overflow sorting and paging. The main entry exports the classic levels and tags, the English strings and the public types. React components follow in a later release.
